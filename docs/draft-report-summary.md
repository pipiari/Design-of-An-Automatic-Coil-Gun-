- A coauthored narrative connecting early experiments, mechanical construction, mobility, visual sensing, and embedded integration.
- Two tables of frame-indexed experimental observations and derived quantities, with associated figures (pp. 21-23).

## Public release treatment

The original PDF contains detailed construction and operational material and is not distributed here. This summary records provenance and evidence limits without reproducing those details or turning the draft into a build guide. No original report text, drawings, or quantitative performance claims are silently revised.

No new physical test is required to document existing work. Useful follow-up material would be the editable report source, original figure files with credits, original observation spreadsheets and videos, and dated records resolving what was completed. These can be reviewed privately for appropriate summaries.
